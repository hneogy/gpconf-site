# Provenance of the vendored vectors

`alpha5.json` is an unmodified copy of `vectors/alpha5.json` from the public corpus
gp-omm-conformance at tag `v0.1.0` (commit `fdf4043`; https://github.com/hneogy/gp-omm-conformance;
concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22867654, version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22867655).

- SHA-256: `9df250841f0667a5466c24d806002b857abb80065fa9fc4641014339dda0933e`
- Copied 2026-09-21 from the local export at commit `78ba712`, verified byte-identical to the tag commit.

It is used by the client-side Alpha-5 tool (self-test) and by the weekly library-status job. It
contains the Space-Track letter table, official examples, boundaries and invalid inputs; it
contains no element sets.
