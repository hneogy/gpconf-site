The world ran out of five-digit satellite numbers. Does your software know?

On 11 July 2026 the satellite catalog reached number 100000. Much older software can quietly lose every satellite catalogued since. This is a free test kit that shows developers exactly where their code breaks.

Read the story Try the tools Test your software See the live tracker

A conformance corpus for parsers crossing the five-digit catalog-number boundary

Seventeen cases built from real CelesTrak responses and published specification vectors: Alpha-5 TLEs, six- and nine-digit NORAD_CAT_IDs, CCSDS OMM in CSV, JSON, XML and KVN, and one writer-side case for tools that produce TLEs. Every expected value traces to a recorded URL, retrieval time and SHA-256. Point the runner at your parser, or at the TLEs your tool writes; it tells you, case by case, what breaks and why.
